Veetr focuses on the essentials: apparent and true wind, speed over ground, GPS position, heading, heel, and connection quality. Data updates in real time and stays local to your device.

The app is a Progressive Web App, so sailors can install it from the browser and keep using it offline after the first load. It is designed around repeated use on the water: large instruments, light and dark modes, quick Bluetooth connection, and layouts that work on phones and tablets.

Unlike many marine electronics systems, Veetr is independent from a boat's electrical system. A standard USB-C power bank can power the prototype hardware for long sailing sessions.
