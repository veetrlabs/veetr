The project has its roots in the Czech Republic. The name comes from the Czech word for wind, “vítr”, where the long "i" is pronounced close to "ee" in English.

The philosophy is simple: knowledge should move freely. Open source code, circuit diagrams, design files, and open data should help sailors, makers, and contributors understand, reuse, and build on the tools they rely on.
