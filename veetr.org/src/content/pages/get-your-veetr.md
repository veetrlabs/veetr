Finished Veetr hardware is not for sale yet. Certification and compliance work for the complete device are the current blocker to sales, with production development and testing still ahead.

Using certified components is a useful starting point, but it does not establish conformity of the finished product. We need to settle the production design, confirm the applicable requirements, obtain lab quotes and fund the work. There is no confirmed launch date or certification budget yet.
