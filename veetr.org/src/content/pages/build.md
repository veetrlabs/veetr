Every important part of Veetr is open: firmware, app source, PCB design, enclosure direction, and documentation. That matters because a sailor should be able to understand, repair, and adapt the instrument instead of being locked into a vendor.

The hardware uses standard modules and parts: ESP32, ultrasonic wind sensor, IMU, GPS, RS485 interface, connectors, and a custom breakout PCB. Builders can source components directly, manufacture or order the PCB, print or adapt an enclosure, and flash the firmware locally.

This is still an early hardware project, so building requires patience and comfort with electronics. The docs are the source of truth, and community feedback is part of how the design becomes better.
