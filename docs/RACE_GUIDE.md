Veetr connects race organisers, sailors, and spectators. You can use race management without owning a Veetr Vane: a phone can provide GPS tracking.

[Open race management](/races/manage/) or [browse public races](/races/).

## Which role do I need?

| Role | What you can do | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Series manager | Configure an assigned series, races, heats, fleet, scoring, and its officials; also run racing. | [Series managers](/docs/race-managers/) |
| Referee | Manage heat entries and results, share results, and control race tracking in an assigned series. | [Referees](/docs/race-referees/) |
| Skipper | Manage a boat’s profile and team, and participate in its race tracking. | [Skippers](/docs/race-skippers/) |
| Crew | Participate in tracking for a boat you belong to. | [Crew](/docs/race-crew/) |
| Spectator | View shared standings, live maps, and available replays. | [Spectators](/docs/race-spectators/) |
| Administrator | Manage users and access across Veetr, with authenticator verification. | [Administrators](/docs/race-administrators/) |

Roles can overlap. Being a skipper does not grant permission to edit race results. Being a referee does not grant permission to manage users. Series roles apply to the assigned series; boat roles apply to the boat across the races it enters.

## Series, races, and heats

A **series** groups races, such as a season championship. A **race** contains one or more **heats**. Record finishes in a heat; race standings combine its heats, and series standings combine race scores.

The series fleet is the pool of boats available to enter races. Adding someone to a boat’s crew is separate from registering that boat to compete.

## Sign in and find your work

Open [Account](/account/), enter your email, and follow the sign-in email. Use the address that received your invitation. **My series** and **My boats** are the starting points for your own items. Open an assigned series through race management.

Public viewing does not require an account. A race-phone invitation can also let a sailor join one race without an account; it does not grant permanent boat or series management access.

## Before race day

Agree on categories, race starting points, discards, and responsibility for recording finishes. Test one phone and the public map together before the fleet leaves. See [Scoring and results](/docs/race-scoring/) for the scoring model.
