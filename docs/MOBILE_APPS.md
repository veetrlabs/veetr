## Availability

Veetr for Android and iPhone is currently available through invitation-only beta testing. The apps are not yet generally released on Google Play or the App Store. This page will link to the public downloads when they are available.

## Request beta access

Use the [contact form](/about/#contact) and say that you would like to test the Veetr mobile app. Include your platform and the email address for your invitation:

- **Android:** send the Google Account email you use in Google Play on your phone. It does not have to end in `@gmail.com`, but it must belong to a Google Account.
- **iPhone:** send an email address where you can receive the TestFlight invitation.

For example: “I'd like to test Veetr on Android. My Google Play account email is …” or “I'd like to test Veetr on iPhone. Please send the invitation to …”. If you want to test both, include both platforms and their invitation addresses.

Access is added manually. After your request is processed, you will receive an invitation or installation instructions.

## Install on Android

1. Request access using your Google Play account email.
2. Open the testing link you receive while signed in with that same Google Account.
3. Join the test, then install Veetr through Google Play.

If the link says you do not have access, check which Google Account is selected. Veetr's test version is not discoverable through a normal store search. See [Google's testing guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334) for how testing access works.

## Install on iPhone

1. Install Apple's free [TestFlight app](https://testflight.apple.com/).
2. Open your Veetr invitation email on your iPhone and choose **View in TestFlight**.
3. Accept the invitation and install Veetr.

Use TestFlight for beta updates. If a build expires or no compatible build is available, contact us. Apple's [TestFlight guide](https://testflight.apple.com/) explains invitations, updates, and feedback.

## After installation

Open Veetr and sign in with your Veetr account to use account features such as boat access and trip sharing. The beta invitation gives you access to install the app; it does not create a Veetr account or grant access to a boat.

To record and publish a voyage, follow [Share your trip](/docs/share-your-trip/). A Veetr Vane is optional for phone GPS recording and supplies additional instrument readings when connected.

## During the beta

The apps are still changing. Features described in the guides may arrive in a later beta build than the one you have installed. Check for updates first; if an option is missing or something goes wrong, use the [contact form](/about/#contact) and include your phone model, app version, and what happened. iPhone testers can also send feedback through TestFlight.

The [browser dashboard](https://app.veetr.org) remains available separately. Its installation and Bluetooth setup are covered in the [web dashboard setup guide](/docs/).
