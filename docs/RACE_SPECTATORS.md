You do not need an account to view shared races.

## Find an event

Open [Races](/races/), search for a series, and open it. Choose a race to see its heats, standings, fleet, and **Map & replay**.

Only shared information appears publicly. A missing race may still be a private draft; ask the organiser for the correct link.

## Read standings

Use the category controls to switch between all boats and a category. Lower points are better. A crossed-out score is discarded from the counted total.

Standings can change while racing is in progress or when the referee corrects a result. A dash means no counted result is available, not necessarily that the boat did not start. See [Scoring and results](/docs/race-scoring/) for details.

## Follow the map

Open **Map & replay** to see available positions. Use **Fit fleet** to bring the boats into view and full screen for more map space.

Check position freshness. A missing or old position can mean a phone is offline, has poor GPS reception, or is not sharing. It does not establish the boat’s current location or race status.

## Replay a race

When recorded tracking is available, use the replay controls to play, change speed, or scrub through time. A replay shows the recorded positions; gaps in tracking remain gaps in the available record.

Race tracking is for following the event. Use the referee’s results for official finishing order.

## Share with others

Copy the series or race page link. Other viewers can open public results without signing in. Opening a public page does not grant access to edit results, contact details, or team management.
